Modes of legitimacy in flamenco periodicals, 1977–1998.

A corpus study of Candil (Peña Flamenca de Jaén) and the Anglophone monthly Jaleo (San Diego), pairing a hybrid retrieval + LLM annotation pipeline with a 180-article human-gold sample and a 163-article LLM-comparison subset that bounds what the pipeline can yet support.

§2Research question
02 / 27
The question

How do specialized flamenco periodicals — Candil, Jaleo, and their peers — construct flamenco legitimacy across Spanish and Anglophone contexts?

A
Through which modes is legitimacy constructed? We posit five: authenticity, heritage, community, pedagogy, criticism.
B
How do these five modes perform authority-work — recognizing, excluding, defining, or delegitimating actors, practices, and forms of knowledge?
C
Do the Spanish-language and Anglophone publications differ in which modes they emphasize, and in where within the periodical those modes are performed?
†
Scope The study describes how legitimacy is constructed in print; it does not adjudicate which constructions are correct.

§3Secondary literature
03 / 27

The five modes are not invented here.

Each mode is anchored in a body of scholarship that frames the concept and supplies its diagnostic features. LEGIT is not a sixth mode: legitimacy is inferred from how the five families authorize, recognize, exclude, transmit, or evaluate flamenco.

AUTH
Bendix (1997) · Handler & Linnekin (1984) · Malefyt (1998) · Wieczorek (2020)
HERIT
L. Smith (2006) · Kirshenblatt-Gimblett (1998) · Cruces Roldán (2014)
COMM
Malefyt (1998) · Washabaugh (1996) · Cruces Roldán (2003)
PED
Casas-Mas (2018) · Pozo & Montero (2008)
CRIT
Washabaugh (2012) · Wieczorek (2020)
LEGIT
analytic effect, not a separate frequency category; the five modes compose the authority-work through which legitimacy is constructed

§3Modes as operations
04 / 27

The modes are operations of legitimacy, not just labels.

Each mode names something a periodical does to flamenco — an act of authorization — not merely a topic it covers.

ModeOperationQuestion it answers
AUTHauthenticatesWho or what is treated as genuine?
HERITpatrimonializesWhat is framed as inheritance?
COMMbinds communityWho belongs to the flamenco world?
PEDtransmits knowledgeWho teaches, learns, or preserves technique?
CRITevaluatesWho has authority to judge?
Legitimacy is the effect of these operations: authenticating, inheriting, gathering, teaching, and judging.

Paratext layer A separate detector links each article to its recurring section — and the two periodicals are built differently. Candil sorts ≈1,518 articles into ~30 genre-differentiated editorial rubrics (Editorial, Ensayo, Discografía, Ponencia/comunicación, Coplas/poemas, Homenaje…), most persisting across the full 1979–1998 run. Jaleo's sections are fewer and functional/community (Juergas, Letters, Announcements, Reviews, San Diego Scene), and 50% of its 1,410 articles fall outside any recurring section. Detection is heuristic, but the structural contrast holds corpus-wide.

Codebook v12.

Entman-style frames as competing modes of legitimacy. Codes use family-prefixed IDs such as AUTH_03 and HERIT_11.

Each entry carries eight fields. The do-not-use-when clauses do the heaviest work — they name the collapse-risks between adjacent families.

The human-gold coding.

The human-gold sample is 180 articles — 90 from Candil, 90 from Jaleo — drawn issue-stratified across the full span and coded by hand against the same codebook the pipeline uses. The LLM-comparison subset is 163 (90 Candil + 73 Jaleo): the articles that also have LLM output.

Reconciled to gold labels. Gold ↔ LLM precision/recall/F1 at family and code level is reported in §6.5 — and it is the reliability bound that decides how far every automated claim can travel.

Human-gold · stratification
180 hand-coded (90 + 90) · issue-stratified · reconciled gold · 163 in the LLM-comparison subset (90 + 73) · 7,087-article corpus ⇒ 2.3% coded by hand.

§6.5Automated annotation
12 / 27

The LLM enters after the human-gold baseline.

Everything so far is human-gold and structural. The automated layer is downstream of that work: first build the corpus and paratext structure, then code the gold sample, then use the LLM at scale, and finally bound it against the 163-article comparison subset.

01
Corpus build
scan → OCR text → article units, with issue metadata retained.
02
Paratext structure
recurring sections and article placement establish the structural layer.
03 · gold
Human-gold coding
180 stratified articles are hand-coded and reconciled before automation is trusted.
04
Retrieval + LLM
keyword retrieval proposes evidence windows; gpt-4o-mini assigns code + span.
05
Validation bound
LLM article labels are compared to gold before trends become evidence.
†
Constraint The LLM never replaces the gold layer: it sees only a codebook entry and a small evidence window, then its article-level labels are tested against human gold before they are used for exploratory scale.

§8Critical reflection
17 / 27

The pipeline produces layered evidence, not a single measurement.

Each layer answers a different question and carries a different failure mode. The critical move is to keep them separated when making historical claims.

LayerWhat it contributesMain limitationClaim status
Archive + OCR corpus Scale, chronology, periodical metadata, issue/article boundaries. Survival, selection, OCR noise, and segmentation choices shape the corpus before coding begins. Context
Paratext + sections Corpus-wide evidence for editorial architecture and recurring rubrics. Section detection is heuristic; Jaleo's looser sectioning is partly a historical feature and partly a detection challenge. Strong, with detector caveat
Human-gold sample Validated semantic labels for 180 articles and the central descriptive findings. Small, stratified sample: balanced enough for comparison, too small for fine period/section estimates. Main evidence
LLM annotation Exploratory scale: candidate trends, failure diagnostics, rerun priorities. Different label prior; AUTH/HERIT recall failures; code-level F1 = 0.23. Bounded exploration
†
Bottom line The pipeline is useful precisely because it shows where automation fails: it turns the LLM into an object of validation, not a substitute for historical judgement.

§8Critical reflection
18 / 27

The historical object is mediated print authority, not flamenco itself.

The evidence supports claims about how two periodicals organized and authorized flamenco discourse. It does not directly measure performance practice, reception, or community life outside the page.

H1
Print elites are overrepresented.
The corpus sees editors, critics, contributors, and institutional voices more clearly than dancers, students, audience members, informal gatherings, or oral transmission outside publication.
H2
Spanish/Anglophone comparison is not symmetrical.
Candil writes from inside an Andalusian institution; Jaleo writes from a diasporic/Anglophone learning scene. Difference in mode emphasis may reflect genre ecology, translation work, and institutional position as much as national context.
H3
Categories are analytic translations.
AUTH, HERIT, COMM, PED, and CRIT help compare discourse, but they flatten rhetoric, irony, genre, and language-specific idioms. Close reading is required before treating a mode as an historical intention.
H4
Section architecture is historical evidence, not neutral metadata.
Rubrics are editorial acts: they sort what counts as review, essay, news, memory, pedagogy, or community. The strongest where finding is therefore about periodical form as authority-work.

§9Future work
19 / 27

Future work: make the pipeline more historical, not just more automated.

Methodological next steps
M1
Validate section detection. Build a section gold set, report precision/recall, and separate real unsectionedness from detector misses.
M2
Expand human gold. Add targeted samples for weak cells: Jaleo named sections, late Candil, AUTH/HERIT-heavy articles, and small rubrics.
M3
Rerun annotation at article level. Let the model read the article first, then ask codebook questions; keep trigger-window labels as a retrieval trace.
M4
Retune retrieval. Use false negatives from the LLM-comparison subset to repair AUTH/HERIT under-retrieval and PED/CRIT over-triggering.
Historical next steps
H1
Return to articles. Pair each quantitative contrast with quoted close readings, especially where the same mode does different authority-work.
H2
Follow actors and institutions. Link authors, peñas, schools, festivals, record labels, and correspondence to the legitimacy modes they mobilize.
H3
Compare peer periodicals. Test whether Candil's dense rubric system generalizes to La Nueva Alborea, other magazines.
H4
Publish the apparatus. Release the codebook, human-gold labels, section schema, and validation reports so the historical claims remain inspectable.
†
Priority The next version should not ask whether the LLM can replace reading. It should ask where computation can point us toward better reading.
